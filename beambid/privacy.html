Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026. Operator: linxitech.

BeamBid keeps its quote workspace on your device. Its code does not automatically upload your quotes, customer names, usage information or identifiers to us. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, cloud synchronization or developer-operated app server.

Data stored on your device

Local data includes business and customer names, project notes, drafts, saved quotes, material and machine profiles, pricing inputs, currency and settings. Migration and recovery can preserve earlier local data. Access depends on your device and operating-system security. We do not hold a server copy of the workspace.

Copies you choose to export or share

Copying, sharing, printing or saving a PDF sends the selected customer quote to the destination you choose. A JSON backup contains the full workspace and can include sensitive customer information and internal pricing inputs. A protected-original export may contain data this version cannot read.

The operating system, recipient, printer or Files provider processes these copies under its own policies. Providers such as iCloud Drive may synchronize exported files. BeamBid does not automatically synchronize its active workspace between devices, and its code does not send us a copy of these exports.

Retention and deletion

Keep a verified backup outside the app before clearing data, resetting the workspace, changing devices or uninstalling. Removing the app deletes its local container subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior. Restoring an older backup can restore previously deleted records. Manage exported, shared and backup copies at their destinations.

Apple services

Apple handles paid downloads, optional In-App Purchases, device backups and optional platform diagnostics under Apple's policies and your settings. Version 1.1 uses Apple StoreKit to verify purchase and original-download access on your device. Purchase verification does not send your quote workspace to us, and we do not operate a receipt-validation server. Apple may provide developers with sales reports, aggregate usage or crash diagnostics. We do not receive payment-card details. These platform services are separate from automatic collection by BeamBid's code.

Support correspondence

If you email 1059891685@qq.com, linxitech receives your address, message and attachments. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and improve BeamBid. Send only information you intend to share; quotes and backups can contain private customer or business data.

We retain correspondence while needed for the request and applicable security, dispute or legal obligations. You may request deletion at the same address; eligible correspondence is deleted after verifying the request unless those obligations require retention. We do not sell support information or use it for targeted advertising.

These web pages

Opening a support or privacy link sends a request through your browser. Hosting services may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the page. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not automatically receive your BeamBid workspace.

Children and changes

BeamBid is a general business utility whose code does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. We will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures if the app's data practices change.

Privacy questions

Contact linxitech at 1059891685@qq.com for privacy questions or support-information deletion requests.
