BeamBid support

Help with laser-job quotes, saved projects and workspace backups.

BeamBid 1.0 · Updated September 10, 2026 · iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.

Make and save a quote

  1. Open Quote, name the project and enter the quantity, materials, time and rates.
  2. Check the total and calculation breakdown, then choose Save quote.
  3. Open Saved and choose Customer view. Copy, share, print or save a PDF when ready. Customer output omits internal costs and profit assumptions.

Check unexpected amounts

Quantity is a whole number. Check whether rates are hourly and times are in minutes, and enter percentage fields as percentages. BeamBid applies waste to material, failure allowance to direct cost, selling fees and target margin to revenue, then discount and tax. The displayed job subtotal is authoritative; rounded average unit prices can differ from the subtotal divided into individually rounded items.

Saved quotes retain their original currency. Confirm the currency of an imported legacy quote before sharing it. Selecting another currency is not an exchange-rate conversion.

Back up before clearing data

In Settings > Backup & restore, export the JSON backup, select a Files location and verify the file exists. Keep a copy outside the app before deleting BeamBid, resetting its workspace or moving devices.

To restore, import a BeamBid backup and review the explicit replacement confirmation. Reopen the app and check the restored data. Importing replaces the active workspace; it does not merge two workspaces.

When saving or importing fails

Keep the draft open and retry after resolving the reported issue, such as device storage. Unsupported, damaged or future-format backups are rejected without replacing the active workspace. If local data cannot be read, export its protected original before confirming a reset.

Exports and file-provider copies are separate from the active workspace. Deleting a quote in BeamBid does not delete copies already shared, printed or exported. Apple or your Files provider may handle device backups and cloud copies under your settings.

Product scope

BeamBid is an offline business quotation and recordkeeping aid. You provide the inputs and choose the final price. It does not control a laser, recommend equipment settings, determine device or construction safety, certify compliance, or guarantee profit.

Purchases and restores in version 1.1

Version 1.1 is being prepared for release. It will include one complete saved quote for free: calculations, editing, customer view, sharing, printing and PDF export. An optional permanent, non-consumable Full Unlock purchase enables additional saved quotes, duplication into new records and multi-quote backup imports. There is no subscription. The currently released paid-download version keeps its existing access.

Customers who bought the earlier paid-download version retain full access. In version 1.1, open Settings and choose Restore purchases while signed into the original Apple Account. Purchasing and restoring access require the App Store and a network connection; quote calculations and locally saved records work offline. A refund does not delete your existing quotes: they remain readable, editable and exportable, while new records above the free limit require access again.

Purchases restore access, not your quote files. Export and verify a workspace backup before uninstalling or moving devices.
