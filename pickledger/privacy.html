Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026. Operator: linxitech.

PickLedger keeps its order library on your device. Its code does not automatically send us orders, barcodes, usage events or device identifiers. It has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or developer-operated app backend.

Local data

The library includes order references, SKU and item names, expected quantities, package codes and units, count/correction events with timestamps, saved import mappings and saved code catalogs. Previous and recovery copies can be retained locally. We do not hold a server copy. Access depends on your device’s security and Apple’s operating system.

Camera

With your permission, Apple’s on-device camera framework reads a barcode and proposes its text for your review. PickLedger does not save camera images or video and does not upload them. You can deny permission and type a code instead.

Files you select, export or share

A selected CSV or JSON backup is read locally. Exports and backups can contain private order information. The destination, recipient or Files provider you choose processes those copies under its own policies. iCloud Drive or other providers may synchronize exported files. PickLedger does not automatically synchronize its active library or send us a copy of your files.

Apple purchases and services

Apple processes the optional one-time Pro purchase, refunds and restore requests. PickLedger uses verified StoreKit entitlement information on device to determine Pro access. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide developers with sales reports, aggregate usage or crash diagnostics under Apple’s policies and your settings. These platform services are separate from data collection by PickLedger’s code.

Retention and deletion

Keep a verified backup outside the app before uninstalling or changing devices. Removing the app deletes its local container subject to Apple’s device backup and restoration behavior. Restoring a backup replaces the library and can bring back older records. Manage exported, shared, device-backup and cloud-provider copies at their destinations. PickLedger has no remote account to delete.

Support messages

If you contact 1059891685@qq.com, linxitech receives your address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate. Send only information you intend to share and avoid confidential orders or full backups.

We retain correspondence while needed for the request and applicable security, dispute or legal obligations. You may request deletion at the same address. After verification, eligible correspondence is deleted unless those obligations require retention. We do not sell support information or use it for targeted advertising.

Support website

Opening these pages makes a browser request. Hosting services may process IP addresses and request logs to serve and protect the website. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts and do not receive your app library automatically.

Children and changes

PickLedger is a general work utility. Its code does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If its data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, email 1059891685@qq.com.
